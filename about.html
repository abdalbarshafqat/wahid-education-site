About us

Hi, we're Wahid Education.

We're a small education company with a simple idea: bring good teaching into the places communities already gather. We partner with community centers and masajid to run weekly, instructor-led classes for kids, teens, and adults, starting with math.

Our mission

To help people improve their knowledge and skills through affordable, practical, and accessible programs, taught in familiar community spaces.

What that looks like
  • Classes meet every week, in a space your family already knows.
  • Every instructor is qualified, and recruited and paid by us.
  • One simple monthly fee, so families know exactly what to expect.
  • We serve Muslim and non-Muslim communities, and shape each program around what that community wants.
